Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 27, 2026

Decent Care ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website DecentCare.in. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure experience for all our users. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and safe-keep your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when filling out our contact forms, requesting home consultation visits, or contacting us via WhatsApp. This may include:

  • Personal details: Your name, phone number, and physical home/site address (for measurements).
  • Preferences: Choice of furniture collections, custom fabric preferences, or interior design needs.
  • Communication Data: Prefilled messages and notes sent via WhatsApp or emails.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used solely to offer premium, personalized services. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Schedule and coordinate home measurement visits by our design experts.
  • Send custom pricing quotes, catalogs, and design drafts based on your requests.
  • Respond to enquiries submitted through our website and WhatsApp desk.
  • Improve website functionality and general customer service.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We value your trust. Under no circumstances do we sell, trade, or lease your personal information to third-party marketing companies. We only share details with trusted members of the MNS Group who assist us in manufacturing, transporting, and assembling your custom furniture, under strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Data Security

We deploy robust electronic and managerial security protocols to secure the data collected online. Our site runs on encrypted SSL streams to ensure that transmission of contact queries is secure.

5. Cookies and Tracking

DecentCare.in uses basic browser cookie structures to optimize loading speeds and remember simple navigation behaviors. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though it may affect page transition efficiency.
