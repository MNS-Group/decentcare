# Decent Care Website — Image Guide

> Add your actual product images into the `images/` subfolders below.
> **File format**: `.webp` (recommended) or `.jpg` — keep file sizes under 200 KB.
> **Dimensions**: Use **1200×900px** (landscape 4:3) for collection cards and gallery.
> Use real product photos against clean backgrounds (white/cream) for a luxury feel.

## Quick Checklist — Recommended Upload Order

1. **Hero banner** — `images/hero/hero-banner.webp` + mobile version (MOST important — it's the first thing visitors see)
2. **Logo** — `images/logo/decent-care-logo.webp` + `decent-care-favicon.webp`
3. **Sofa** — At least `premium-sofa-set-01.webp` (best seller, main product)
4. **Collection cards** — One image per product category (fill missing cards with your actual product photos)
5. **Gallery** — At least 6 images in `images/gallery/`
6. **Teakwood** — `teakwood-furniture-01.webp`

Once you add images, the placeholder `📷` indicators will disappear automatically.

## Photography Tips for Luxury Feel

- **Lighting**: Bright, well-lit photos. Natural daylight preferred.
- **Background**: Clean, uncluttered backgrounds. White/cream/light grey.
- **Angle**: Slightly elevated angle (45°) shows depth best.
- **Resolution**: Minimum 1200px wide. Don't upscale small images.
- **File size**: Compress to under 200 KB per image (use tinyjpg.com or squoosh.app).
- **Consistency**: Use similar lighting and background across all product shots.
