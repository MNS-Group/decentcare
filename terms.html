1. Custom Orders & Design Approvals

All furniture units manufactured by Decent Care are custom-made to order. Once design blueprints, fabric selections, and dimensions are explicitly approved by the client (following measurement visits), changes to the structural framework or upholstery color cannot be accommodated.

2. Doorstep Measurement Visits

We provide doorstep measurement visits within Hyderabad. While our design experts will guide layout planning and note clearances, final physical space feasibility rests on the site coordinates supplied by the client during site preparation.

3. Guarantees and Warranties

Decent Care offers structural guarantees on solid wood frames, including teakwood structures sourced from permitted government reserves in Chhattisgarh and Maharashtra. Warranty details on mechanical fixtures, hydraulic jacks, gas recliners, and upholstery fabrics are provided on the invoice document.

4. Pricing and Payments

Pricing estimates are valid for 15 days from the date of quotation. A standard advance payment is required to initiate procurement and carving processes, with the remainder payable upon delivery/installation at the client site.

5. Delivery and Installation

Delivery dates are approximate estimates. While we aim to fulfill orders on schedule, handcrafting premium teakwood and PVD steel units requires detailed processes that may occasionally result in minor extensions. We coordinate carefully to ensure clean, on-time installation.
